🧪 Research projects

Current and related work

How can heat acclimation move from the laboratory into everyday working conditions? My doctoral research focuses on practical protocols using heart-rate-prescribed exercise and protective equipment, alongside the physiological measurements needed to evaluate adaptation.

Determining exercise intensity under protective equipment

Identifying a heart-rate-based exercise intensity that induces a meaningful and tolerable increase in core temperature while wearing protective clothing.

Practical heat acclimation

Investigating whether repeated exercise in protective equipment can induce heat acclimation without relying on traditional climatic-chamber protocols.

Heat acclimation markers

Assessing physiological and thermoregulatory adaptations following practical heat acclimation, including core temperature, heart rate, sweating responses, and heat tolerance.

Predicting core temperature responses

Exploring interindividual variability in core temperature responses during exercise under controlled heart-rate prescription and protective equipment.

Validation of sweat onset threshold measurement

Evaluating the agreement between the Hygrochron iButton and ventilated capsule technique for determining sweat onset threshold during passive heat stress.
